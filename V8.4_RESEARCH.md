# V8.4 — Exact batch-event portfolio research

V8.4 investigates a different question from the V5/V8 probability models: for a fixed batch of ten six-number tickets, what fraction of all possible six-number draw outcomes produces at least one ticket with 3+, 4+, 5+, or 6 hits?

## Method

- Enumerate the full outcome space C(45,6) = 8,145,060; no Monte Carlo approximation is used for the reported batch probabilities.
- Compare the frozen V8.1 ten-ticket reference with 19 deterministic candidate structures generated from distinct cycle/spectrum fingerprints of simple 3-regular graphs on ten vertices.
- In each candidate structure, the 15 graph edges represent 15 numbers shared by exactly two tickets, while each of the ten vertices receives three private numbers. Therefore all 45 numbers appear, each ticket contains six distinct numbers, every pair of tickets overlaps in at most one number, and the mean pairwise overlap remains 1/3.
- Optimize exact 3+ batch coverage first and exact 4+ batch coverage second. Report 5+ and jackpot as guardrails rather than tuning targets.
- Compare with the exact analytical baseline for ten distinct tickets sampled uniformly without replacement from all 8,145,060 possible tickets.

## Evidence boundary

This is batch-structure optimization only. It does not use historical draws, does not fit number probabilities, does not increase the theoretical chance of any one fixed ticket, and does not establish predictive superiority. It must not change V5 production selection or any forward-lock commitment. The existing draw-1252 lock and candidate file are untouched. Any future use with model-weighted numbers requires its own prospective validation.
